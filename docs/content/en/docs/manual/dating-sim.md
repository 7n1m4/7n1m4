# Dating Sim & Interactive Scenarios

Anima's Dating Sim Engine (visual-novel gameplay, intimacy and mood mechanics, branching choice cards, and scene overlays) is implemented in the stage-ui dating-sim store and overlay components.

See the code-colocated [Dating Sim & Interactive Scenarios](https://github.com/7n1m4/AnimA/blob/main/packages/stage-ui/src/stores/DATING-SIM.md) document for entering a scenario, core mechanics, the four mood states, branching choices, and scene backgrounds.
